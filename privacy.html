Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Fliqs ("we," "our," or "the app") is a social streaming companion that helps you track shows and movies, connect with friends, and discover new content. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who can see it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and display name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google. If you sign in with Apple, we receive your name and email from Apple (Apple may provide a private relay email if you choose to hide your address). We do not store your Google or Apple password. We also store your device's time zone so that daily features, reminders, and release dates display correctly for your location; this is a general time zone (e.g., "America/New_York"), not precise location data.

Profile Information

You may provide a username, bio (favorite quote), profile emoji, and profile photo. Profile photos are uploaded to our storage servers and are accessible via a public URL. Your profile includes a privacy toggle that controls whether your activity feed is visible to non-friends and whether you appear in user search results.

Watch Activity & Ratings

We store information about the shows and movies you track, including your watch status (watching, finished, watchlisted, dropped), episode-level watch progress, ratings, start and completion dates, rewatch counts, and your ranked top picks. We also store when you dismiss episode reminder cards from your home screen.

Social Activity

We store your friend connections, best friend designations, posts, comments, reactions, @mentions, blocked user relationships, and direct title recommendations you send or receive to other users.

Reports and Requests

If you report content or another user, we store the report — including your account identifier, what you reported, the reason you selected, and any additional detail you choose to provide — so that our team can review it. If you submit a request for a streaming service to be added, we store the service name and any note you include, along with your account identifier, so we can prioritize which services to support.

Games & Trivia

When you play games or daily trivia, we store your submissions including guesses, whether you solved the puzzle, time taken, hints used, and your leaderboard ranking. Your display name and avatar appear on public leaderboards.

Preferences

We store your selected streaming services (e.g., Netflix, Hulu, Max) and optional taste preferences from the onboarding questionnaire.

Recommendation Analytics

To improve content suggestions on your "For You" feed, we track which recommendations you see, which you interact with, and outcomes such as whether you added a recommended title to your list or rated it. We also track when you dismiss a recommendation and the reason you provide (e.g., "not interested," "already seen").

Device Information

If you enable push notifications, we store your device's push notification token and platform (iOS or Android). We do not collect device identifiers for advertising purposes.

Local Device Storage

We store authentication tokens, UI preferences (such as feed filter settings, recent searches, and tutorial completion), and temporary game state on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers beyond what is described above.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Precise location data
  • Contacts or phone numbers
  • Financial or payment information
  • Device identifiers for advertising

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain the app's features including tracking, social feed, discovery, games, and personalized recommendations
  • Display your profile to other users based on your privacy settings
  • Show you where titles are available to stream based on your selected services
  • Send you alerts about new episodes, friend requests, comments, reactions, and other social activity
  • Generate leaderboards for trivia and games
  • Improve recommendation quality based on your interactions and feedback
  • Review reports of content or users, and take action on violations of our Terms of Service

Who Can See Your Information

Public Profiles

If your profile is set to public, other users can find you in search and view your profile information, watch activity, top picks, badges, stats, and activity feed.

Private Profiles

If your profile is set to private, you will not appear in user search results. If another user navigates to your profile (for example, from a friend request you sent), they can see your basic profile information (name, username, bio, avatar) but they cannot see your watch activity, top picks, stats, badges, or activity feed posts unless they are an accepted friend.

Trivia & Game Leaderboards

Your display name and avatar are visible on daily trivia leaderboards regardless of your privacy setting.

Profile Photos

Uploaded profile photos are stored at publicly accessible URLs. Your profile photo is displayed wherever your avatar appears in the app, including friend lists, activity feeds, comments, alerts, and leaderboards.

Reports

Reports you submit are visible only to our moderation team, not to other users or to the person you reported.

Content Moderation

Fliqs includes social features where users can post, comment, and interact. We do not tolerate objectionable content or abusive behavior. You can report any post, comment, or user profile from within the app, and you can block users. We review reports and act on violations — including removing content and, where warranted, suspending or removing accounts — within 24 hours. See our Terms of Service for the full rules that govern acceptable use.

Third-Party Services

Fliqs uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Our backend provider for authentication, database, file storage, edge functions, and push notification relay. All user data described in this policy is stored on Supabase servers. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • The Movie Database (TMDb): We use the TMDb API to provide movie and TV show information, images, cast details, and streaming availability. No user account information is sent to TMDb — only content queries such as title searches and media IDs. This product uses the TMDb API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDb. TMDb Privacy Policy
  • JustWatch: Streaming availability data (where titles can be watched) is sourced from JustWatch via TMDb. JustWatch Privacy Policy
  • TVMaze: We use the TVMaze API for accurate episode air dates. No user account information is sent to TVMaze — only show identifiers and name queries. TVMaze Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In: If you choose to sign in with Google, authentication is handled through Google's OAuth service via Supabase. Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Sign-In: If you choose to sign in with Apple on iOS, authentication is handled through Apple's sign-in service. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Expo Push Notifications: Push notifications are delivered through Expo's notification service using your device token. Expo Privacy Policy

We do not currently use third-party analytics, advertising, or crash reporting services, and we do not sell your data to third parties. If we introduce features such as affiliate links to ticketing or commerce partners in the future, we will update this policy to disclose any associated third-party tracking before those features become active.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, which provides encrypted connections and row-level security policies to restrict data access. Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device. While no system is 100% secure, we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your Choices

  • Set your profile to private so you don't appear in search and your activity feed is hidden from non-friends
  • Edit, hide, or delete your posts and comments at any time; hidden posts are only visible to you
  • Report posts, comments, or user profiles that violate our Terms of Service
  • Remove friends from your connections
  • Block users to prevent them from finding you in search or sending friend requests; blocking also removes any existing friendship
  • View and manage your blocked users from the settings screen
  • Delete your account directly from within the app, with the option to either permanently erase all of your data or deactivate your account while preserving your data for a future return

Blocking

When you block a user, any existing friendship is removed, and neither of you can find the other in search or send friend requests. The blocked user will no longer see your activity feed, posts, or comments, and you will no longer see theirs. Historical comments or reactions made before the block may remain in the database but are hidden from both users' feeds going forward. You can unblock users at any time from the Blocked Users section in your settings.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the settings screen within the app. When deleting your account, you have two options:

  • Delete account and all data: Your account and associated data — including your profile, watch history, ratings, posts, comments, reactions, friend connections, game submissions, trivia answers, badges, alerts, streaming preferences, recommendation data, reports you have submitted, push notification tokens, and uploaded profile photos — will be permanently removed. Aggregate statistics (such as community game scores) that do not identify you individually may be retained. This action cannot be undone.
  • Deactivate account (save data): Your account will be deactivated and your profile will no longer be visible to other users, but your data will be preserved. If you sign back in with the same account in the future, your account and data will be reactivated.

You can also contact us at fliqssupport@gmail.com to request account deletion.

Children's Privacy

Fliqs is intended for users aged 17 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at fliqssupport@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the app or by updating the date at the top of this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at fliqssupport@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Fliqs ("we," "our," or "the app") is a social streaming companion that helps you track shows and movies, connect with friends, and discover new content. Your privacy matters to us, and this policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and display name. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email from those services. We do not store your Google or Apple password.

Profile information: You may optionally provide a username, bio, profile emoji, and your streaming service preferences.

Usage data: We store information about the shows and movies you track, your watch progress, ratings, activity posts, comments, reactions, and friend connections. This data powers the core features of the app.

We do not collect:

  • Location data
  • Contacts or phone numbers
  • Financial or payment information
  • Device identifiers for advertising purposes

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the app's features (tracking, social feed, discovery)
  • Display your profile to friends and other users (based on your privacy settings)
  • Show relevant content such as streaming availability and new releases
  • Send alerts about friend requests, comments, and activity on your posts

Third-Party Services

Fliqs uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Our backend and database provider, which stores your account and app data securely. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • The Movie Database (TMDb): We use the TMDb API to provide movie and TV show information, images, cast details, and streaming availability. This product uses the TMDb API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDb. TMDb Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In: If you choose to sign in with Google, your authentication is handled by Google. Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Sign-In: If you choose to sign in with Apple, your authentication is handled by Apple. Apple Privacy Policy

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, which provides encrypted connections and row-level security policies to ensure users can only access their own data. While no system is 100% secure, we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your Choices

You can:

  • Set your profile to private so only friends can see your activity
  • Edit or delete your posts and comments at any time
  • Remove friends or block users
  • Delete your account directly from within the app, with the option to either permanently erase all of your data or preserve your data for a future return

Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the settings screen within the app. When deleting your account, you have two options:

  • Delete account and all data: Your account and all associated data (watch history, posts, comments, friend connections, and profile information) will be permanently removed. This action cannot be undone.
  • Delete account but save data: Your account will be deactivated and your profile will no longer be visible, but your data will be preserved. If you create a new account with the same email in the future, your previous data can be restored.

You can also contact us at fliqssupport@gmail.com to request account deletion.

Children's Privacy

Fliqs is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the app or by updating the date at the top of this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at fliqssupport@gmail.com.

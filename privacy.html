Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

Fliqs is a social companion for tracking shows and movies. It is operated by Jonah Walker ("Fliqs," "we," or "us"). This policy explains what we collect, who can see it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. Our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines sit alongside this policy.

Information we collect

Account

When you create an account we collect your email address. If you sign up with email and a password, our authentication provider stores the password as a hash. We do not receive your Google or Apple password. If you use Google Sign-In, we receive your name and email from Google. If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive your name and email from Apple. Apple may give us a private relay address if you hide your email. A sign-in session is stored on your device.

We store the fact that you accepted the Terms, and whether the account is deactivated.

Profile

You can add a display name, username, bio, profile emoji, and eye color, and you can set the profile to public or private. You can also choose whether your name appears on trivia leaderboards, within the age rules below.

Profile photos and banner images are stored in a public storage bucket. Any signed-in user can see your profile photo on your card. Your banner is part of your full profile, not the card. Because the files are public, anyone who has the file address can open the image, including outside the app. We ask for camera or photo-library access only so you can choose an image to upload. We do not upload your camera roll.

Age

We collect your birth month and year, not the day. We use them to confirm you are 13 or older and to apply the teen rules. They are visible to you and to us. They are not shown on your profile or on leaderboards. You can change them only to a younger age.

Apple's age range may be used in a future update to apply these rules. That feature is not live, and we do not collect an Apple age range today.

If a signup attempt says the person is under 13, we delete any account created for that attempt. That device then stores a block for 30 days so the signup cannot be repeated right away. The block is a timestamp on the device only. It does not include a birth date, email address, or account id. Uninstalling the app removes it. It also expires on its own after 30 days.

Watching

We store the titles you track and the status you set (watchlist, watching, finished, or dropped), episode checkoffs, a progress summary, title ratings, and season ratings. Reviews you write are stored as posts, including the text, a rating, and whether you marked a spoiler.

We store the streaming services you select, and the answers you give on the optional taste questionnaire. Those answers are stored for personalization. Recommendation scoring does not use them today.

We store your device's time zone name, such as America/New_York, so "today" and the 8pm trivia reminder match your local day. This is not GPS, and it is not your precise location.

Social

We store friend connections, best-friend marks, and blocks. We store posts, comments, likes, and mentions of friends you tag. We store posts you hide from your own feed. If you send someone a title, we store the title, who sent it, who received it, and any note you include. If you follow a cast member, we store that person's catalog id, name, and photo path.

Trivia, leaderboards, and kudos

We store your trivia answers and your own trivia stats. The leaderboard shows rank and time. It shows your name and profile photo unless your result is anonymous. Kudos are short reactions on a finish. We store who sent one, who received it, and the reaction. Counts appear on the leaderboard. If you are not anonymous, the recipient's alert includes your name. If you are anonymous, the alert says "A trivia player" and does not include your account.

Recommendations

Fliqs Picks stores the titles we showed you, how they were ranked, which friends they relate to, a score, and the kind of impression. It also stores what you did afterward, such as watching, finishing, or rating, and the path you took, such as search or a friend. If you mark a card "not interested," "already seen," or "bad match," we store that feedback.

If you open a title from someone else's post, we store that you opened it and whether you later finished or rated it. We use that for badges. It is not a public feed.

Search

People search and title search are used to run that search. We do not keep a search history. Title search text is sent to TMDb for that request. The search tab keeps the current session in memory and clears it when you sign out.

Reports

If you report a post, comment, or profile, we store your account, the target, the reason, any detail you add, and a snapshot of the content at that time. Our moderation team can see reports. We do not tell the reported person who filed the report. We also keep a moderation log of actions we take.

Device and notifications

If you allow push notifications, we store that device's Expo push token and whether it is iOS or Android. Push is tied to the device. Signing out of Fliqs on a phone stops notifications on that phone and removes that phone's token. Tokens on your other phones stay until you sign out there or delete the account. Deactivating the account does not, by itself, delete tokens on other devices.

If you turn on Siri, that iPhone keeps a token in its keychain. Our server stores a hash of the token, a device label, and the last time it was used. The label is the phone's model plus a short random suffix. It is not the name you gave the phone. We do not let the app read the hash back. Siri search text is sent to TMDb for that request and is not stored on your account. We store a short receipt of a Siri action so a retry does not run it twice. Turning Siri off, or signing out, revokes Siri on that phone. Deactivating does not revoke Siri on your other devices.

The iPhone widget keeps a copy on that device of show names, the next episode, progress, poster images, your account id, and any "marked watched" actions waiting to sync. Fliqs does not put that copy on our servers or in iCloud. Signing out clears it.

Information we do not collect

  • Precise location or GPS
  • Contacts or phone numbers
  • Payment information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • A third-party analytics or crash-reporting kit

We do not show ads, we do not sell your information, and we do not use third-party tracking. Supabase and Expo keep their own operational logs of requests they handle. Those logs are described in their policies, linked below.

How we use information

  • Run your account, sign-in, and the age rules
  • Show your profile, posts, and watch activity to the people allowed to see them
  • Track what you are watching and show where a title may be available
  • Send alerts you have turned on, including episode reminders, friend requests, comments, and kudos
  • Run trivia, leaderboards, and kudos
  • Build Fliqs Picks recommendations and award badges
  • Review reports and enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines
  • Remember that you accepted the Terms

Who can see it

You

You can see your own account, including your birth month and year, streaming services, taste answers, watch progress, trivia stats, Fliqs Picks activity, and recommendation activity.

A card every signed-in user can see

Any signed-in user can see a card with your name, username, profile emoji, and profile photo, including when your profile is private. Bio is not on that card.

Search returns that card. People 16 and older can be found by a partial name or username. Ages 13–15 appear only when someone types their exact username. Search leaves out deactivated accounts, suspended accounts, and anyone blocked in either direction.

Full profile, watch activity, and posts

Your full profile includes your bio, banner, eye color, and similar profile fields. It is visible to you, to accepted friends, to any signed-in user if your profile is public, and to a person you have sent a pending friend request.

Watch status, ratings, progress summaries, and the posts on your profile follow the same rule: you, your friends, or any signed-in user if the profile is public. Episode-by-episode checkoffs are stored as your progress and are not a separate public list. Friends can also see your posts in their home feed. A title recommendation is visible to you and the person you sent it to.

Ages 13–15 are private and cannot switch to public.

Friends, blocks, and cast

A friendship or a block is visible to the two people involved. Blocks are not a public list. Blocking removes the friendship and hides you from each other in search and in the feed. Marking a best friend sends that friend an alert. Cast you follow is visible only to you.

Leaderboards and kudos

Any signed-in user who is not blocked or suspended can open the leaderboard and see rank and time. Name and profile photo are shown unless that player is anonymous. An anonymous player appears as "Anonymous Player" with no photo. You still see your own row.

This follows the anonymity setting, not the public or private profile switch. Ages 13–15 are always anonymous and cannot turn that off. Ages 16–17 start anonymous and can opt out, which shows their name and photo. Adults start visible and can choose to be anonymous.

Kudos counts are visible with the leaderboard row. The sender's name is visible to the recipient unless the sender is anonymous. Ages 13–15 can receive kudos only from friends.

Us, and not other users

Birth month and year, streaming services, taste answers, push tokens, Siri token hashes, Siri action receipts, Fliqs Picks signals, and recommendation clicks are for operating the app. Other users do not get a feed of them. Reports and the moderation log are visible to our moderation team, not to other users, and we do not tell a reported person who reported them.

Teens

Fliqs is for people 13 and older. We delete accounts that are under 13. After an under-13 signup attempt, that device is blocked from another signup for 30 days, as described above.

  • Ages 13–15. The profile is private and cannot be made public. Leaderboards are anonymous and that cannot be turned off. Other people can find the account only by the exact username. Kudos can come only from friends.
  • Ages 16–17. Leaderboards are anonymous by default. You can opt out and show your name and photo.
  • Adults. Leaderboards show your name by default. You can switch to anonymous.

Apple's age range may be used in a future update. It is not live.

Deactivate or delete

You can do either from the app, or email support@fliqsapp.com and ask us to delete the account.

  • Deactivate hides your profile and keeps your data. You disappear from search, and the profile is set to private. Posts and other information stay stored. Signing in again reactivates the account. Reactivation does not turn the profile public again. It stays private until you change it, and ages 13–15 stay private either way. Deactivating does not remove push tokens or Siri access on other devices. Signing out of a device stops push and Siri on that device.
  • Delete is permanent. We remove the account and the information tied to it, including your profile, watch history, posts, friends, trivia, recommendations, reports you filed, push tokens, and Siri data. Two limits: moderation log entries are kept, with your account removed from the record of who acted, and those entries are not shown to other users. Deleting your profile photo and banner is best-effort. If that cleanup fails, the account is still deleted, and the file may remain in storage until it can be removed.

How long we keep it

We keep the information in this policy for as long as the account exists. Deactivating keeps it. Deleting removes it, except for the moderation log and a photo file that storage cleanup could not remove.

We do not keep title-search text or Siri search text as a history. Fliqs Picks activity and Siri action receipts stay until you delete the account. We do not run a separate expiry job for them. The on-device under-13 block lasts 30 days or until you uninstall the app. The widget copy is cleared when you sign out of that phone.

Third parties

We use the following services. We do not show ads, we do not use third-party tracking, and we do not sell your information.

  • Supabase hosts authentication, the database, file storage, and the server functions that run Fliqs. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • TMDb supplies title information and images. Title search, including Siri title search, sends the search text to TMDb. Image requests load artwork from TMDb. We do not send your Fliqs profile to TMDb. This product uses the TMDb API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDb. TMDb Privacy Policy
  • TVMaze supplies episode air dates. We send show identifiers and name lookups, not your account. TVMaze Privacy Policy
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple, and Siri on your iPhone if you turn it on. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In handles Google sign-in if you choose it. Google Privacy Policy
  • Expo delivers push notifications and app updates. A push uses your device token. An update check tells Expo which app version to send. Expo Privacy Policy
  • Resend sends report notifications, including report details, to our moderation inbox, and sends you email if we remove your content or suspend your account. Resend Privacy Policy
  • YouTube plays trailers. Starting a trailer loads the video from YouTube, and YouTube receives that playback request. Google Privacy Policy
  • Streaming apps and sites open when you follow a link out of Fliqs. From that point, their own privacy policy applies. We do not use affiliate links today.

Your choices and rights

  • Set a profile to private. Ages 13–15 are private and cannot change that.
  • Choose leaderboard anonymity where your age allows it. Ages 13–15 stay anonymous.
  • Edit your profile, delete your own posts and comments, and hide a post from your own feed.
  • Remove a friend, or block someone. You can review blocks in settings.
  • Turn push notifications off, or sign out of that phone to stop them there.
  • Turn Siri off. That revokes Siri on that phone.
  • Skip the taste questionnaire. It is optional.
  • Deactivate or delete the account in the app.
  • Ask us for access to the personal information we hold, or ask us to correct it, by emailing support@fliqsapp.com. We may need to confirm the request is yours.

US state privacy rights

Depending on the state where you live, you may have the right to know, access, correct, or delete personal information we have about you, and to appeal if we deny a request. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Email support@fliqsapp.com to use these rights. A parent or guardian of a teen user can use the same address.

Security

Fliqs stores account data with Supabase, using encrypted connections and row-level access rules. Sign-in tokens stay on your device. No service is perfectly secure. We take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app. If a change requires a new agreement, the Terms describe when we ask you to accept it before you continue.
